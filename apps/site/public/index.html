nema

your learning state, everywhere.

The web teaches. Your vault remembers. Your agent connects the two.

A vault you own holds signed evidence of what you have learned. Websites never read it: they ask one question, and the vault answers with the smallest true answer, after you approve it. Every page offers its capabilities as WebMCP tools, so there are no accounts and no shared database.

How it works

  1. 1 LearningManifest

    A provider publishes what a unit teaches, what it assumes, and what evidence it can produce.

  2. 2 ReadinessAssertion

    Your vault answers one request with bands only, signed, after you approve it in a modal.

  3. 3 EvidenceReceipt

    You do the work, the provider signs the result, and your vault recomputes from the ledger.

  4. 4 LearningNeed

    The vault turns the ledger into what to do next, with the rubric attached.

Privacy you can see

The token does not exist until you click Approve, and it carries only the bands that were asked for, bound to one origin for 30 minutes.

Five tools nema does not have, on any surface:

  • set_mastery state is derived from signed evidence, never written.
  • get_full_history disclosure is per request and per audience, never bulk.
  • submit_answer_for_learner only the human answers, and grading runs server side.
  • disable_review the schedule is a property of the evidence, not a setting.
  • export_vault export is a button a human clicks, not an agent capability.

Check it on any nema page with await document.modelContext.getTools().

For judges

  • ChatGPT desktop. WebMCP is on by default, so the tools are discovered with no setup.
  • Chrome 149 or later. Enable chrome://flags/#enable-webmcp-testing, restart, open the coach.
  • Any other browser. Every page loads the WebMCP polyfill and behaves identically.

What a ReadinessAssertion carries:

  • Minimal. Three lines requested, three lines shared.
  • Audience bound. Valid at one origin. Elsewhere it fails with wrong-audience.
  • Short lived. 30 minutes, no refresh.
  • Unlinkable. learnerKeyId is derived from your key and the audience, so two providers see different ids.
  • Refusable. Deny returns { status: 'denied' } and writes nothing.
