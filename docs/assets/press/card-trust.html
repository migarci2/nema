Trust has three tiers

Who signed a receipt changes what it is worth

registered a key listed in shared/issuers.json full weight
origin a key published at /.well-known/nema-issuer.json full weight
self a key made in the reader's browser, nothing else capped at 0.3

A fourth state, pending, is what a receipt gets when no key matches it at all: it is kept and shown, and it moves nothing until a key turns up.
